Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

  1. Open Police Radio Scanner+.
  2. Go to the screen that shows the Restore Purchases button.
  3. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy, then tap Restore Purchases.

Restoring usually takes a few seconds, but it can take up to a minute on a slow connection. If nothing is restored, email us at appsinsights@gmail.com.

How do I use a Redeem Code from support?

You can enter the code that support sent you in one of two ways:

  • In the app: tap the Redeem Code button and enter it.
  • In the App Store app: tap your profile picture at the top of the screen, then tap Redeem Gift Card or Code and enter it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Choose Police Radio Scanner+.
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Please note: deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Where can I find the privacy policy?

You can read it here: https://wearefaces.github.io/privacy/police/. It is also linked in the App Store listing and in the app.

There is no sound, or a feed doesn't load. What can I do?

  • Check the volume and make sure the silent switch is off.
  • If you use Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, check that audio is going to the right device.
  • Check your Wi-Fi or mobile network connection.
  • Feeds are live streams run by volunteers, so a feed can go offline for a while. Try another feed.
  • If the problem continues, email us at appsinsights@gmail.com and include the name of the feed.

Which areas are covered?

Feeds are available for the United States and Canada only.
