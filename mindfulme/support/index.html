FAQ

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Mindful Me and go to Settings → Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → Apple ID (your name) → Subscriptions, select Mindful Me and tap Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
